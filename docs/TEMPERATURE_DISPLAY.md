# Seasonal temperature display

All supported 850-mb and 2-m temperature-anomaly maps use one shared −6°C to +6°C scale in 0.5°C discrete bands. Every boundary, including ±0.5°C, is labeled on the colorbar and has a distinct display color; no half-degree band is rendered as pure white. The palette follows the approved muted 500-mb reference scale: dark blue through pale blue and gray near zero, then pale rose through dark red.

The 24 colors align with 25 boundaries. Values outside the scale saturate at the endpoint colors; forecast values and downloaded numerical grids are unchanged. The renderer samples the full projected frame, including its small visual padding, so a finite global source field reaches the map spine without extrapolating data. Monthly and seasonal products use the same scale, colorbar formatting, CONUS frame, and renderer. Existing images acquire the new style when rerendered. The temperature-style-refresh workflow refreshes supported products and publishes successful producer runs.
